# Brand reference

Source: four user-supplied OHSU Brand Hub screenshots captured September 24, 2026 (Typography and Icons, Principal Brand Logo, Sub-brand Logos, and Colors).

The Colors screenshot's Cool palette reads:

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charcoal | #545859 | Body text, neutral footer |
| Blue | #0057B7 | Links, buttons, feature panel |
| Light Blue | #4698CA | Decorative panel edge only |

Lato is used for headings and interface text; Noto Serif for editorial copy. White and #f4f6f7 are neutral project surfaces. #d5dcdf is a decorative divider. These last two values are not claimed as official brand tokens.

The screenshot appears to repeat the blue hexadecimal value under the green swatch despite showing a different RGB value. This starter avoids the unused green palette rather than silently resolving that discrepancy. Additional warm and hot palettes are intentionally omitted.

Logo and sub-brand screenshots were reviewed only as constraints. No logo, derivative mark, or official lockup is used. The favicon is a plain letter P. Brand screenshots are not redistributed in this public package.

## Homepage alignment update

Layout reference: https://www.ohsu.edu/ (reviewed September 28, 2026). Adopted its blue navigation, full-width hero, rounded yellow call to action, overlapping three-column white panels, and blue section headings. The navigation tone #124c80 and dark gradient endpoints are project adaptations; Horizon yellow #ffc72c comes from the supplied logo-color reference. No OHSU photography, logo, institutional navigation, or institutional claims were copied. A blue gradient stands in for photography until suitable group imagery is available.

## User-specified UI colors

Primary UI blue is #0e4d8f (navigation, footer, primary links and headings). Rounded pill buttons use linear-gradient(to bottom,#ffc529,#ffd769). These replace the earlier adapted navigation tone and flat yellow button treatment.

Text links on white/light surfaces use #0057B7 with #003f85 on hover. Header/footer links remain white with gold hover and no added background; gradient buttons retain dark text. Primary section headings remain #0e4d8f.

Headings on light backgrounds use the user-specified #585e60. Linked headings retain #0057B7 and hero headings remain white.

## Homepage typography update

At the user's request, typography now follows rendered styles from https://www.ohsu.edu/ (September 28, 2026): Lato throughout; desktop hero 62px/1.21 at 700 weight; navigation 18px regular; pathway headings 35px regular; section headings 42px light; feature headings 48px light; body 16px/24px; footer 14px/21px. Values are expressed in rem. Mobile hero is 24px, pathway headings 22.5px, and feature headings 36px. Line-height is adapted where necessary for multi-line headings. This supersedes the original serif editorial treatment. Colors and logo restrictions are unchanged.

H2 line-height is 1.2 across viewport sizes, tightened at the user’s request for multi-line headings.

Pill gradient direction: to top by default; to bottom on hover, with stops #ffc529 and #ffd769.
